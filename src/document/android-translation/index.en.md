Android post translation in DisFly is powered by **Google Translate** using **Google ML Kit** on-device language identification and translation models. Translation works independently of the AI setting, including on devices that do not support Gemini Nano.

## How to translate a post

- Tap the translation icon in the post toolbar to translate into your device language. Tap it again to show the original.
- Press and hold the translation icon to choose a target language. You can turn off automatic translation in the General section of Settings.
- DisFly may need to download language packs over Wi‑Fi on first use. Once the packs are ready, you can translate already-loaded posts offline.

## How DisFly handles your text

Translation runs on your device. DisFly does not send post text to a cloud translation service for this feature. Images and link destinations are preserved. If the download or translation fails, the original remains available, and DisFly does not fall back to generative AI.

## Attribution and accuracy

Translations on Android are generated automatically by Google Translate. The Google Translate badge appears beside the translated content. Machine translation can get names, forum jargon, and context wrong. Check the original when the details matter.
